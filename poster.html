🎯 Problem & Motivation
Clinical Need

Menstrual cycles naturally fluctuate due to hormonal variations, stress, and conditions like PCOS and PCOD. For married women planning pregnancy, accurately identifying the 6-day fertile stage and 12–24h peak ovulation viability window is crucial. Existing tracking apps often compromise privacy by uploading sensitive health telemetry to remote clouds.

Sakhi addresses this challenge through a 100% private, client-side application powered by classical computer science algorithms to deliver precise cycle forecasting and pregnancy planning.

🔒 Privacy & Data Integrity
